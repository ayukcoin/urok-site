Urok

An in-house content studio by AyukLab

Urok is the tool AyukLab uses to plan, make and publish short videos, reels and illustrated books for its own projects, such as the game Loktak Lake: Match 3 Puzzle.

It is used only by the AyukLab team. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Turns a story idea into a short video with illustrations, narration and music.
  • Groups the work into projects, each with its own channels and posting calendar.
  • Uploads finished videos to AyukLab's own YouTube channels and Facebook pages, at times the team chooses.
  • Shows simple numbers for those channels, such as subscribers and views.

Connecting a channel

When a channel owner connects a YouTube channel or Facebook page, they sign in with Google or Facebook and approve access themselves. Urok uses that access only to upload the videos the owner schedules and to read the channel's basic statistics. Full details are in the privacy policy.
