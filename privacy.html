Privacy Policy for Urok

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Urok is an in-house tool run by AyukLab (“we”) for making and publishing videos on AyukLab's own channels. It also covers AyukLab's internal workflow automations that sign in with the same Google app. It is used only by the AyukLab team; it is not offered to the public.

The short version

When you connect a YouTube channel or Facebook page, Urok stores a sign-in key so it can upload the videos you schedule and read basic channel statistics. We do not sell, share or advertise with this data, and you can disconnect at any time.

What we access and why

YouTube (Google). With your permission, Urok uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos you have chosen to publish, with their titles, descriptions and tags (youtube.upload);
  • read your channel's name, ID and public statistics, such as subscribers and views (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).

Facebook (Meta). With your permission, Urok lists the pages you manage, publishes videos and posts you schedule to the pages you pick, and reads their basic engagement (pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, publish_video).

We do not read your private messages, comments, contacts, email or anything else outside these purposes.

What we store

  • The access and refresh keys from Google or Facebook, encrypted before they are saved.
  • The channel or page name and ID, and the statistics listed above.
  • A record of each scheduled or published post: when, where, and its result.

This data is kept on AyukLab's own server. Only the AyukLab team can reach it.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, except when sending your videos to YouTube or Facebook as you asked, or when the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Urok's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Urok uses YouTube API Services; by connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Keys and channel details are kept while the channel stays connected.
  • When you disconnect a channel in Urok, Urok stops using its keys straight away.
  • You can also remove Urok's access yourself at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Facebook in Settings › Business integrations.
  • To have all data about your channel deleted, email us and we will do it within 30 days.

Children

Urok is a tool for adults on the AyukLab team. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
